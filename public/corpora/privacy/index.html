Corpora Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026 · Applies to the Corpora iPhone app (listed on the App Store as “Corpora 3D”) · Publisher: Michael Kushman

Summary

Corpora processes everything on your iPhone. The publisher does not collect, receive, or store any of your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no app-operated server.

What the app stores, and where

The following are stored only in the app’s local storage on your iPhone:

  • Captures you record or import (video, photos, and any depth data your device provides)
  • Frames the app extracts from those captures
  • The 3D models (USDZ files) the app generates
  • Artwork details you type in: title, artist, material, and notes

None of this is sent to the publisher or to any server operated by the publisher.

How 3D reconstruction works

Models are built on the device using Apple’s RealityKit photogrammetry. No reconstruction happens off the device.

Camera and imported media

The app uses the camera to record captures of your sculpture. If you choose to import photos or video, the app uses only the items you select.

Exporting

When you export a model, Corpora hands the USDZ file to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example Files, Mail, or AirDrop). Once exported, that copy is handled by the destination you picked and its own privacy terms.

Tracking, analytics, and advertising

Corpora contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code. Its bundled privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data.

Keeping and deleting data

  • The library does not currently have a control for deleting an individual sculpture.
  • Saved inputs from failed or cancelled builds are intentionally kept so they can be recovered.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from the iPhone. Copies may remain in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup), according to your device’s backup settings.

Children

Corpora does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
