    Before a session: a two-minute checklist
    • Text people first, not during. Anyone who might expect to hear from you gets one message now: "offline until [time], all good."
    • Set your person above — sitter, partner, or the clinic line — so the call button is there if you need it.
    • Do Not Disturb on. An incoming text you can't answer well is temptation you don't need.
    • Lock through the afterglow. Judgment comes back after the body does — give it a couple of hours past when you expect to feel normal. There's an "add one more hour" button if you're unsure later.
    • Want hard enforcement too? See "Make it stick at the phone level" below.
    • Water nearby, playlist ready. Nothing you write tonight needs to send tonight.

    Your record

      Make it stick at the phone level

      TextLock is a commitment device — a promise with a timer and some friction, kept honestly. It cannot reach into your phone's messaging app. If you want hard enforcement for the same hours, set it up once in your phone's own controls:

      • iPhone: Settings → Screen Time → App Limits → add Messages (and WhatsApp, etc.), or turn on a Focus that silences and hides them. Ask someone you trust to hold the Screen Time passcode.
      • Android: Settings → Digital Wellbeing → App timers → set a timer on Messages, or use Focus mode to pause it for the evening.
      • Both: start TextLock for the same window so you have a countdown, a place to park almost-sent texts, and a record of the locks you kept.
